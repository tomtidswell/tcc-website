FEEDBACK for 2021

What do we do well?

 Rehearsals are good under the circumstances. Enthusiasm is high considering we’re not working towards a performance.

 I’m very happy to have these weekly rehearsals. I feel closer to the members that come and also enjoy the WhatsApp conversations. I love the themes.

 Rehearsals work really well. It isn't easy managing technology at the same time as engaging everyone's attention and keeping us all going. Thanks to Nicki; I think the fact that we've kept going in strange and challenging circumstances is a great thing.

 I really look forward to singing on a Tuesday and like the ‘live’ format with Nicki every week. I very much appreciate Nicki’s input this year which has lifted my spirits in an isolating time and I enjoy ‘seeing’ other people on Zoom and knowing they are well.

 I think the current format has worked well and keeps our voice muscles in practice regularly.

 The fact that we're managing to keep going at all is great, with membership of 40+ and regular attendance of 30+.

 It's good to be able to sing together, in a sense, and good that we all take it seriously.

 It’s great that rehearsals have continued throughout the lockdown & restrictions. It’s lovely to be able to see each other & have a chance to chat. I’ve enjoyed the different monthly “themes”, & the range of music we’ve sung.

 I really appreciate the choir is carrying on online and the work you are all doing to enable this. Nicki is doing an amazing job keeping us all upbeat and energised.

 I’m grateful that rehearsals have continued on Zoom; Nicki does a wonderful job of lifting our spirits on a Tuesday night. It obviously works well socially, and even if I don't contribute it's really nice to see everyone.

 The warm-ups work well and give us a chance to really use our voices - important if we're going to sing together again one day!

 Thirty plus people on zoom every Tuesday.

 Managing to keep the choir going during lockdown.

 I like Nicki’s upbeat and forever optimistic approach - it’s a bit of a laugh.

 I appreciate being able to join just for a short time – I’m usually too tired in the evening to stay for long.

 I enjoy ‘seeing’ so many of the choir each week. It’s good to know you are all there and OK. I also appreciate the bit of time before the start for a chat, and though I seldom say anything it’s a little more connection.

 Being able to see other choir members really helps during these difficult times.
​
 It’s so good to sing and see everyone. Nicki keeps me smiling.

How could we improve things?

 Working on pieces with harmony and sections would be good if we always had one on the go, with midi files of parts etc., even if we can’t perform them all together. Singing in unison is good for warm-ups but not very challenging.

 I find it difficult to do harmonies as I need to hear other people around me to help me keep in tune which is impossible until we meet in person again.

 Not sure how other choirs manage to practise live. Maybe we could explore software that allows for this better than Zoom.

 Not sure about TV themes – not keen on Dukes of Hazzard, Baywatch, Big Bang Theory.

 Simple harmony would definitely be good, sometimes at least, with more specific practice of those particular songs.

 It would be good to have another go at recording.

 Can starting notes be reiterated for each section? I always end up tuning into Nicki.

 Possibly breakout rooms sessions for smaller groups – not sure how this works.

 Maybe we could make the announcement of themes a more regular thing and people could give suggestions in time for Perf and Rep to discuss in the meetings? But as it is, it’s lovely.

 I would like the chance to do some basic harmonies at some point because it's a reminder of what it’s like to not sing the main tune. It's good practice.

 Practising simple harmony is good, though it doesn’t really work online. I don’t think there is much more Nicki can do because of the technology constraints.

 Simple harmony (e.g. singing a third above) good occasionally, also simple rounds. At least you can hear yourself singing in contrast to piano/Nicki.

 Simple harmony could be good – perhaps a little more variety in the kinds of songs we sing.

 Singing songs in unison is probably best for me as I do need to hear the rest of my section to be able to feel confident with harmony.

 I enjoyed continuing to learn repertoire at the beginning and didn't mind having a go at recordings, but I know I'm in the minority here. Could we perhaps work at something for a bit longer than we do with a very little harmony, as well as singing things for fun?

 I miss getting to the point where I can really sing something - probably just me as I don't know many of the songs! I've also enjoyed revisiting old rep.

 Revisiting pieces from previous zoom sessions, and having less new songs.

 Singing in unison or simple rounds works best. I find singing even simple harmony difficult without being able to hear the rest of my section.

 Harmonies are difficult without being able to hear other choir members. I tend to forget the harmony! I really prefer familiar pieces that I can just sing along to.
