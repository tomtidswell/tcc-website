### 3. ABOUT US

**URL:** `https://www.tottenhamcommunitychoir.org/about-us.html`

#### Choir Motto

"Singing Makes You Feel Good!"

#### About the Choir

- Practice: Every Tuesday evening
- Members put hearts, souls, and voice boxes into feeling good
- Musical range: Wide selection from many countries and genres
- Performance opportunities:
    - Real range of venues
    - Interesting events through the year
    - Regular Tuesday evening rehearsals

#### Learning Approach

- Generally begin learning from sheet music (but not always)
- Aim: Enable singers with all levels of musical experience to achieve their best
- Focus: Finding pleasure of singing within large group

**Sheet-Music-Free Performances (since 2012):**

- Decision made in 2012
- Goal: All public performances sung without sheet music wherever possible
- Benefits:
    - Transformed the choir and its sound
    - Makes choir more whole, responsive, and together
    - "A real part of what makes TCC unique as a community choir"
- Strength comes from unity

#### Musical Director

Nicki Williamson, Musical Director Tottenham Community Choir

#### Vocal Structure

- Four vocal parts: Soprano, Alto, Tenor and Bass (SATB)
- Uncertainty welcome: "Don't worry if you're not sure which part your voice is best suited to - just have a seat and join in the warm up!"
- Nicki helps members decide where they're most comfortable singing
- Guest copies of music scores available

#### Images

- Choir performance photo included
- Image URL pattern: `https://www.tottenhamcommunitychoir.org/uploads/4/3/9/1/43916423/`
