# Member Resources

## Performance Dress Code

Guidelines for what to wear during performances will be posted here closer to each concert.

### General Guidelines

Please ensure your performance attire is:
- Clean and well-presented
- Comfortable for singing
- Appropriate for the venue
- Consistent with choir guidelines provided before each performance

Specific requirements (colors, formal/casual, accessories) will be communicated by email and discussed at rehearsals before each performance.

## Serving Refreshments

### Tea Break at Rehearsals

We have a tea break during our Tuesday rehearsals where hot drinks and cake are available at 50p each.

**Remember to bring your own cup!**

### Refreshment Duty

Members take turns helping to serve refreshments during the break. This involves:
- Setting up drinks
- Serving cake
- Taking payments (50p per item)
- Cleaning up after the break

### Rota

The refreshment duty rota will be posted here and shared via email. If you're unable to make your assigned week, please swap with another member and let the committee know.

Thank you for helping to keep our tea breaks running smoothly!
