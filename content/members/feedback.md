# Feedback

We value your feedback and suggestions! Your input helps us improve the choir experience for everyone.

## Share Your Thoughts

Please send your feedback, suggestions, or concerns to:
- **Email:** comms@tottenhamcommunitychoir.org
- **In person:** Speak to any committee member at rehearsal

## Topics

We welcome feedback on:
- Repertoire choices
- Rehearsal format
- Performance venues
- Social events
- Communication
- Any other aspect of choir life

All feedback is appreciated and will be considered by the committee.
