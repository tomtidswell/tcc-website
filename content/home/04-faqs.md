## Frequently Asked Questions

::Accordion{title="What makes TCC special?"}
We sing a wide selection of music from many countries and genres, with lots of opportunities to perform at interesting venues and events throughout the year.

One thing that really sets us apart: in 2012 we decided to perform without sheet music wherever possible. While this can be a challenge at first, it's transformed our sound and unity as a choir. It makes us more responsive, more together, and creates a unique connection with our audiences.

We welcome singers of all experience levels. While we often begin learning from sheet music in rehearsals, we work to ensure everyone – from complete beginners to experienced musicians – can achieve their best.
::

::Accordion{title="Which vocal part should I join?"}
The choir is split into four vocal parts: **Soprano, Alto, Tenor and Bass (SATB)**.

Not sure which part suits your voice? Don't worry! Just have a seat and join in the warm-up. Nicki is always happy to help you find where you're most comfortable singing. Guest copies of music scores are available.

**We're especially keen to welcome tenor and bass singers** to help balance our sections.
::

::Accordion{title="What happens at my first rehearsal?"}
At your first visit, a committee member will welcome you, show you where to sit, and introduce you to members of your section. We'll make sure you feel comfortable and know what's happening.

We have a tea break during rehearsals. A hot drink and piece of cake are available at 50p each. **Please bring your own cup.**

**Please contact us before your first visit** so we can give you a proper welcome!
::
