# About Tottenham Community Choir

**"Singing Makes You Feel Good!"**

That's our motto, and every Tuesday evening the members of Tottenham Community Choir put their hearts, souls, and voice boxes into feeling good.

## About TCC

Tottenham Community Choir (TCC) was established in 2009 by local residents, with the help of a Making The Difference grant from Haringey Council, to provide opportunities for people in Tottenham and Haringey to meet their neighbours and sing together.

New and experienced singers of all ages and voices are welcome. We are a **non-audition choir, open to all**, and we sing a wide range of musical styles from classical, to pop and traditional folk songs.

**We are an inclusive choir and welcome a diverse membership in terms of gender, ethnicity, age, disability, religion, sexual orientation and education.**

## Our Approach to Singing

We sing a wide selection of music from many countries and many genres, and have lots of opportunities to sing in a real range of venues and interesting events through the year as well as our regular Tuesday evening rehearsals.

When we learn pieces, we generally (although not always) begin learning from sheet music, but we aim to enable singers with all levels of musical experience to achieve their best and really find the pleasure of singing within a large group of people.

### Singing Without Sheet Music

In 2012 the choir decided that all our public performances would be sung without sheet music wherever possible. Whilst at first this can be a challenge for some of us, it has really transformed the choir and the sound it makes.

Our strength comes from our unity, and singing without sheet music is an entirely different experience. It makes us more whole, more responsive, and more together. It has become a real part of what makes TCC unique as a community choir.

## Leadership

**Nicki Williamson** has led the choir since 2011 and continues to encourage and inspire members to develop their voices and exceed their own expectations.

The choir is managed by a committee elected by the membership. The committee work with Nicki on performance and repertoire choices, manage membership, finances, events and publicity as well as providing strategic leadership on the direction of the choir and its place in the wider Tottenham community.

## Vocal Structure

The choir is split into four vocal parts: **Soprano, Alto, Tenor and Bass (SATB)**.

Don't worry if you're not sure which part your voice is best suited to - just have a seat and join in the warm up! Nicki is always happy to help you decide where you are most comfortable singing. Guest copies of music scores are available.

# How to Join

## Get in Touch

If you are interested in joining us please contact [comms@tottenhamcommunitychoir.org](mailto:comms@tottenhamcommunitychoir.org) for more details.

::alert{type="warning"}
We are currently especially pleased to welcome people who sing **tenor or bass** to help balance the sections.

**Please do NOT attend rehearsals without contacting us first.**
::

::alert{type="info"}
We ask that you **NOT attend if feeling unwell**.
::

## Rehearsal Details

We rehearse every **Tuesday from 7:30 PM to 9:30 PM**.

Your first two rehearsals are **FREE** to give you the chance to decide if the choir is for you.

At your first rehearsal you will be welcomed by a member of the committee who will show you where to sit and introduce you to members of your section.

### Tea Break

We do have a tea break. A hot drink and piece of cake are available at 50p each.

**Please bring your own cup.**
